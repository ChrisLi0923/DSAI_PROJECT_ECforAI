## Defensible positioning

The closest combined overlap is EvoPrompt's search machinery plus prior format/option-order sensitivity and robustness-oriented prompt selection. Our narrow experimental question concerns an all-five-presentations-correct objective, canonical identities, a random-search control, equal logical target-call budgets, selection-validation, and withheld presentations on a frozen 3B model. This is a controlled course study, not evidence of first invention of any component. Failure to improve is informative.

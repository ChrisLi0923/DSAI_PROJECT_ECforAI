# Verified primary-source literature register

Twenty-four relevant2024–2026 public research works. Conference/journal status and preprints are distinguished; this is not a claim that all24 are peer-reviewed publications. All records and the stated mechanisms were checked against primary pages on30October2026. Numerical gains from these papers are not treated as expected project outcomes.

Older foundational references are separately excluded from this recent count.

## [1] Connecting Large Language Models with Evolutionary Algorithms Yields Powerful Prompt Optimizers (EvoPrompt)

Qingyan Guo, Rui Wang, Junliang Guo, Bei Li, Kaitao Song, Xu Tan, Guoqing Liu, Jiang Bian, Yujiu Yang. ICLR2024; preprint first2023.

https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/9156b0f6dfa9bbd18c79cc459ef5d61c-Abstract-Conference.html

LLM operators act on discrete instructions; our studies separate prompt fitness from executable query policy.

ArXiv title begins EvoPrompt; proceedings title differs.
